Make Room: common room nudge prototype

The common room from the ceiling camera's view. People walk in through the entry, sit, and sometimes spread out with bags, laptops, their feet, or by lying across seats. When someone can't find a seat because of that, the wall screen shows a message and plays a soft chime. There's no spoken audio. Tap Turn sound on to hear the chime, and tap any seated person to make them spread out or sit up.

Detector log

    Demo timings are shortened. Planned real values: waiting 10s, over-occupied 30s, miss after 2 min, cooldown 10 min. A miss gets logged and is never repeated. On the real device, a pose model (MediaPipe/YOLO) produces these same seat states, and the video is discarded as it's processed.
